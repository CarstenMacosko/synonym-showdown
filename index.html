Synonym Showdown

Name a synonym… or call a bluff. A free online multiplayer synonym game you play in your browser.

How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same starter word, like happy or obstinate.
  2. On your turn, name a synonym nobody has said yet before the timer runs out.
  3. Think the last player made one up? Challenge them!
  4. A real synonym knocks out the challenger; a bluff knocks out the bluffer. Last one standing wins.

Ways to play

  • Play Friends: share a room code or invite link (2–6 players, no account needed).
  • Ranked 1v1: get matched with players near your rating and climb the leaderboard.
  • Vs. Bots and Pass & Play on one device.
  • Score Attack, Survival and the Daily Word for solo practice.

300 starter words and over 27,000 synonyms, graded from closest to distant. Great for vocabulary practice and classrooms.
